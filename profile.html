Drea Wichman

Seeking a GRC placement in London for 2027

Drea Wichman is an American cyber security professional focused on governance, risk, and compliance. CompTIA Security+ and A+ certified, she is based in London and studying for an MSc in Information and Cyber Security at Royal Holloway. With a humanities background and twelve years managing organic farm operations, she brings hands-on experience with regulatory compliance, risk assessment, and business continuity. Her current work lives at Fenn Agritech, a fictional UK company running a real security program mapped to ISO 27001 and UK GDPR.
